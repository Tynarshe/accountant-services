Year end

Your calm, clear year-end checklist

The practical steps that make finalising annual accounts less daunting and more predictable.

24 May 20244 minute read

Running a business is easier when you know what is coming and what good preparation looks like.

Start with a clean picture

Bring your records, key dates and open questions together in one place. A short, organised review now can prevent a lot of back-and-forth later.

Clarity is not about doing everything at once. It is about knowing the next right thing to do.

Turn the deadline into a plan

Work backwards from the date that matters. Leave time for missing information, review and any questions that need an expert answer. The strongest process is usually the calmest one.

  • Confirm the period and filing deadline.
  • Reconcile the core financial records.
  • Collect explanations for anything unusual.
  • Ask for help early if something is unclear.

Keep the next year easier

Once the work is complete, note what slowed the process down. Small changes to bookkeeping, document storage or monthly reviews can make next year significantly smoother.
